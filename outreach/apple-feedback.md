# Apple Product Feedback Draft

## Suggested destination

Submit through Apple Product Feedback for iPad and iCloud, then reference the public PIXIE research repository. Do not submit private collaborator material.

## Subject

Cognitive accessibility: system-managed creative file naming and recovery

## Feedback

Creative workflows on iPad require people to interrupt their work to remember filenames, folders, versions, and storage locations that the operating system can often derive from context.

This creates a cognitive-access barrier, particularly for people affected by attention, memory, fatigue, or executive-function demands. The problem is not limited to display or input accessibility: it concerns whether the operating system preserves intent and helps a person resume after interruption.

We propose an iPadOS Device Steward interface that lets a person describe a file in ordinary language while the system suggests a filename, version, project location, and provenance entry. It should support natural-language retrieval, show why a result matched, preserve task state, preview organizational actions, and make every change reversible. Learned preferences must remain visible, correctable, exportable, and deletable.

We are documenting this as the PIXIE Device Stewardship hypothesis and beginning consent-first testing with independent musicians, filmmakers, designers, and game developers. We would welcome guidance on the appropriate Apple accessibility, Files, iCloud, Shortcuts, and developer-feedback channels for a reproducible design proposal.

This is an independent accessibility research project and is not affiliated with Apple.
