# PIXIE Partnership Brief

## Contribution

Dominique Devereaux is the founding systems architect and maintainer of PIXIE. The project offers lived expertise, documented design decisions, an accessibility-first architecture, and a privacy-protected case study. Participation must not reduce that contribution to anonymous feedback, inspiration, or unpaid testimony.

## Potential partnership lanes

| Lane | Useful contribution from a partner | PIXIE contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Product engineering | Prototype review, implementation feasibility, device and account boundaries | Workflow architecture, failure cases, synthetic scenarios |
| Accessibility and HCI | Study design, accessible evaluation, publication standards | Lived-experience expertise, research questions, co-design |
| Academic research | Ethics/IRB path, methods, participant protection, analysis | Case material under consent, provenance method, research collaboration |
| Community co-design | Recruitment ethics, facilitation, accountability | Accessible tools, documentation, reciprocal learning |

## Initial bounded ask

A 30-minute private review to identify the appropriate lane, decision-maker, and next evidence requirement. A warm introduction is a successful outcome.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No private health records in the public repository or public demo.
- No use of private correspondence or third-party creative work without permission.
- No diagnosis, treatment, or crisis inference by the prototype.
- Separate consent for research, sharing, publication, AI training, and attribution.
- Clear retention, deletion, withdrawal, and breach-response terms.
- Authorship and compensation discussed before substantive contribution.
- Public claims must distinguish documented evidence, personal account, inference, and proposal.

## Before paid work

Define deliverables, ownership, licensing, credit, publication review, payment timing, expenses, accessibility support, and cancellation terms. Because income and assets can affect public benefits, consult a qualified benefits counselor before accepting compensation or changing business structure.
