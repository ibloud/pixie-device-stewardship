# Practitioner Reference Summary

**Status: PROPOSED**

This document is a structured reference to a voluntary narrative exercise. It is not a diagnosis, clinical conclusion, medical record, risk assessment, treatment recommendation, or finding about the person.

## Mandatory advisory

> This material summarizes a generated narrative exercise and the person's voluntary responses. It does not establish or infer clinical status, diagnosis, risk, trait, condition, or treatment need. It is not a clinical finding or substitute for professional evaluation. A qualified healthcare professional or trained practitioner must determine whether and how this material is relevant to their work.

## Allowed sections

### NARRATIVE EXERCISE

A record of the generated story or an explicitly authorized excerpt.

### SELF-REPORTED

Only the person's own words. Do not convert those words into diagnostic, psychological, or emotional conclusions.

### STORY ARTIFACT PROPERTIES

Only deterministic properties of the generated narrative. Each item should identify the cards, positions, rule(s), and resulting engine action where applicable.

### CONSENT CONTEXT

Record only the consent actions necessary to establish why this document exists and whether sharing was authorized. Do not infer consent from participation.

## Prohibited sections

The system must not generate:

- “patterns requiring evaluation”;
- clinical hypotheses;
- psychological interpretations presented as system findings;
- inferred emotional states;
- diagnostic suggestions;
- treatment recommendations;
- automated risk classifications.

## Transmission

Creation requires C3. Transmission requires C4 and a named recipient or destination confirmed immediately before transmission. A practitioner reference summary is not transmitted merely because it was created.

## Deletion

The local copy can be deleted when the system controls it. Deletion of a transmitted copy is outside the system's control and must not be represented as accomplished.
