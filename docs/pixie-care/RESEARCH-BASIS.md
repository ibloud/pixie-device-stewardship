# PIXIE Care Research Basis

**Status: PROPOSED / NOT VALIDATED**

This document separates evidence about studied behaviors from design analogy and untested claims about PIXIE Care.

## Affect labeling

The cited affect-labeling literature studies direct verbal labeling of emotional stimuli or participants' own emotional responses under specific experimental conditions. It does not establish that reading a generated narrative and optionally naming something recognized in that narrative is equivalent to those experimental paradigms.

Therefore:

- **DOCUMENTED:** affect-labeling research reports effects under the conditions studied.
- **DESIGN ANALOGY:** PIXIE Care includes an optional prompt inviting a person to name what they recognize in a story.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** whether narrative-mediated reflection constitutes affect labeling in the studied sense, and whether it produces comparable effects, is untested.

The system must not claim that PIXIE Care reduces distress, regulates emotion, or improves clinical outcomes.

## Emotion differentiation

Research concerning emotion differentiation may inform the general idea that more specific reflection is worth studying. It does not demonstrate that a five-position narrative spread improves emotion differentiation.

The five-position spread is designed with the intention of inviting more specific self-reflection than a single undifferentiated prompt. Whether this produces emotion differentiation in the sense studied in the literature is untested.

## Exposure-therapy studies

Studies conducted in exposure-therapy contexts should remain background literature unless a future evidence review establishes a direct relevance. Live exposure, physiological measurement, and clinical treatment context differ materially from the proposed narrative exercise.

Those studies do not validate PIXIE Care and must not be used as direct evidence that the PIXIE interaction has equivalent effects.

## Game-based clinical precedents

Game-based clinical products demonstrate that a digital intervention can be rigorously validated under appropriate clinical and regulatory processes. That precedent does not validate PIXIE Care. The format alone confers no clinical evidence.

## Population limits

Where a cited study uses healthy adults, older adults, or a specific clinical population, that population must be named. Evidence from one population must not silently be presented as evidence for another.

## Evidence vocabulary

- `DOCUMENTED` — directly supported by a preserved source.
- `SELF-REPORTED` — the person's own account.
- `ATTRIBUTED` — a claim explicitly attributed to its source.
- `DESIGN ANALOGY` — a proposed relationship between a studied mechanism and a design feature; not validation.
- `HYPOTHESIS` — a claim requiring testing.
- `PROPOSED` — an unvalidated design decision.

## Required future validation

Before making any claim about outcomes, the project would need a separately designed and appropriately reviewed study of the actual PIXIE interaction, population, measures, consent process, and comparator conditions. Until then, product language remains descriptive rather than efficacy-oriented.
