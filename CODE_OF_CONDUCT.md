# Community Conduct

PIXIE welcomes disagreement about its hypothesis, evidence, methods, risks, and proposed platform changes. Participation is judged by behavior—not institution, ideology, status, diagnosis, identity, or whether a contributor agrees with Loptr Lab.

## Constructive participation

- Address a documented claim, method, design, or source.
- Distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Explain the harm or tradeoff you believe a proposal creates.
- Offer a correction, counterexample, alternative, or test when possible.
- Respect consent, privacy, and the right not to disclose health information.

## Unacceptable participation

- harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, or unwanted private contact;
- slurs, identity attacks, dehumanization, or sexualized abuse;
- diagnosing another participant or declaring them incapable, unstable, dishonest, or dangerous based on disability or communication style;
- publishing private files, messages, health information, or identifying case material;
- impersonation or false claims of institutional authority;
- repetitive disruption after a moderation decision;
- attempts to recruit others to overwhelm a person or project;
- instructions to self-harm or content encouraging harm.

Criticism of PIXIE, Made Sick, Loptr Lab, Apple, another provider, or an institution is not itself misconduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may label, hide, lock, limit, remove, or report content and may block accounts when necessary for safety or continued project operation. Moderation actions should use the least restrictive effective response.

Material criticism removed because of abusive delivery should be summarized without the abuse when doing so is safe and useful. Threats, private information, and targeted abuse will not be republished merely to prove they occurred.

See [MODERATION.md](MODERATION.md) for intake, documentation, appeals, and maintainer wellbeing.
