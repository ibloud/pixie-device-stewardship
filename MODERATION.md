# Shielded Participation and Moderation

## Purpose

PIXIE needs evidence from supporters, skeptics, platform employees, researchers, and institutions. It does not require Dominique Devereaux—or any individual maintainer—to personally absorb hostility in order to preserve that evidence.

## Separation of functions

1. **Intake:** structured issue forms collect claims and evidence.
2. **Triage:** a maintainer or trusted moderator applies a label without debating the contributor.
3. **Review:** substantive claims are evaluated against sources and methodology.
4. **Decision:** the project records accept, revise, test, decline, or out-of-scope.
5. **Appeal:** a different reviewer should assess an appeal when available.

The project should add at least two trusted moderators before running a large public campaign. Dominique should not be the required first reader of unfiltered reports.

## Triage labels

- `evidence`
- `counterexample`
- `provider-response`
- `methodology`
- `accessibility`
- `privacy-and-safety`
- `needs-source`
- `needs-consent-review`
- `constructive-disagreement`
- `moderator-review`
- `resolved`
- `declined-with-reason`

Labels classify work; they must not classify people as “negative,” “hostile,” “unstable,” or “bad.”

## Decision record

For a material moderation or research decision, record:

- date;
- public issue or discussion URL, when safe;
- rule or evidence standard applied;
- action taken;
- substantive argument preserved in neutral language;
- appeal status; and
- reviewer.

Do not copy threats, slurs, private information, or graphic abuse into the public log. Preserve necessary evidence privately with restricted access and a retention limit.

## Institutional participation

Representatives of Apple, Google, Microsoft, healthcare organizations, government bodies, advocacy groups, or other institutions are welcome. They must disclose the organization they represent when speaking in an official capacity. Employment alone does not prove that a statement represents the employer.

Institutional status does not grant edit rights, bypass evidence requirements, or exempt conduct from moderation. Opposition from an institution is recorded as a position, not treated as authority that automatically resolves the hypothesis.

## Maintainer protections

- Turn off direct-message intake for moderation disputes.
- Use GitHub issues and the published project email only.
- Do not require immediate responses.
- Batch moderation at scheduled times.
- Let a trusted moderator hide abusive content before the project lead reviews the substantive summary.
- Lock threads that become repetitive or unsafe after stating the reason.
- Use GitHub interaction limits during coordinated disruption.
- Escalate credible threats or unlawful content to the platform and appropriate qualified support.

## Appeals

A contributor may open one appeal explaining the specific moderation rule they believe was applied incorrectly. Appeals must not repeat removed abuse or identify private parties. The appeal outcome and reason are recorded once; repetitive appeals may be closed.

## Legal boundary

This policy is a project participation standard, not legal advice and not a promise to retain every submission indefinitely. Content may be restricted where necessary to protect privacy, safety, intellectual property, platform compliance, or lawful process.
