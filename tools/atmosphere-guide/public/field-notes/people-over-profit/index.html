People Over Profit

Funding independent infrastructure without making participation a commodity.

Infrastructure has recurring costs. People should not become the product simply because those costs need to be paid.

Our growth proposal and Play It Forward PDS entry ask how an artist can keep identity and creative choices independent of a provider. The same principle must govern how we finance that independence.

What must be paid for

Hosting, storage, transfer, backup, recovery, security, accessibility testing, support, moderation and paid operator time are real costs. Include taxes, fees, renewals and replacement reserves. A donation or a grant is not recurring operating revenue unless its terms and duration establish that.

A sustainable mix

  • Keep the public documentation and open-source tools accessible without purchase.
  • Offer optional paid installation, configuration, maintenance and consulting under separate agreements.
  • Evaluate independent grants, including NLnet, subject to current eligibility, licensing and conditions.
  • Evaluate voluntary contributions and transparent shared infrastructure fees; never sell membership, authority, personal data or preferential access.

Atmosphere Money and Atmosphere Tickets are proposed revenue-supporting services, not proven sources of operating income. PIXIE Holdings is the existing business-facing assessment surface. Preserve the no-sponsorship, no-affiliate and no-paid-placement policy.

Financial gates for growth

Use actual pilot receipts and measured operator hours to establish recurring cost and a reserve. Compare recurring unrestricted funding against recurring obligations; publish aggregate costs without exposing private financial records. Agree a service limit, recovery targets and an exit reserve before admitting other accounts. Pause expansion if maintenance or recovery cannot be funded. No price, revenue, grant award or capacity is claimed here.

Fair collaboration

Public review is voluntary. Any commissioned work requires a separate agreement before work starts, with scope, payment, credit and exit terms. Partners should be non-exclusive, respect privacy and portability, and disclose their responsibilities and costs. Participation does not imply employment or future pay.

Questions for review

What funding mix has actually sustained your independent service? How do you pay operators fairly while keeping participation accessible? What reserve and exit commitments should a small PDS operator publish?

Explore the creative use case in Play It Forward, and its Field Notes archive. We welcome corrections from independent artists, operators and developers.
