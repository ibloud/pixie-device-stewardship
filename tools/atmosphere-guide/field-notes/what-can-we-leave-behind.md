# What Can We Leave Behind?

Field Note 015 · November 23, 2026

A person arrives through a song. They follow a remix, find a show, and remember the friend who first passed them the track. Which part belongs in the archive? Maybe the artist credit. Maybe the story of that friendship. Maybe they just wanted to hear the music. They get to decide.

When we talk about leaving sickness behind, we mean the practices a community can choose to stop repeating. Asking someone to tell a painful story again to qualify for help. Treating availability as proof of commitment. Giving the person who can pay more the loudest voice. Someone living with illness or disability belongs here on an ordinary day, with exactly the energy they have.

Imagine a Mayhem playtester has an hour to give. They spot a rule that is hard to read, suggest a clearer version and go home. The next team can carry the change forward, credit them as agreed, and leave their private reasons out of the record. The work survives their departure. They can come back for the pleasure of playing.

That is a reason for this studio to exist. We can earn a living making things easier to use and still make it easy for people to leave, recover, change direction or start something of their own. An archive of life has room for the chapter after the crisis. What would you like yours to remember?
