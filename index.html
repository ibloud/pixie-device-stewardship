The PIXIE promise

Support that respects variable capacity.

01

No punitive streaks

Missed a session? That's information, not failure. PIXIE picks up where you are, not where it thinks you should be.

02

No hidden inference

PIXIE uses preferences you state. It does not diagnose, infer emotion, monitor you secretly, or decide you're struggling without asking.

03

No trapped choices

Every suggestion is explainable. Every action is reversible. You can undo, correct, and delete. The exit is always in the same place.

Consent is an interface

Four visible states.
One person in control.

PIXIE carries Made Sick's safety grammar into device stewardship. Social contact stays closed by default; sharing and participation are separate choices.

For streamers: this is the difference between a tool that helps you and a tool that performs helpfulness at you while quietly doing things you didn't ask for.

  1. 01
    Recognize

    See a cue or pattern without being enrolled.

  2. 02
    Participate

    Choose the scope, duration, and data involved.

  3. 03
    Transmit

    Share only through a separate, explicit action.

  4. 04
    Withdraw

    Pause, export, correct, or delete without penalty.

Phase 2 · private by design · not yet built

A health record is not a public profile.

PIXIE can help a person understand what sources are connected, whether information may be stale, and what they want to do next — without copying medical records into a public site or treating a coded status as a diagnosis.

01

Check the source

Show who supplied a record and when it last synchronized. Expired access is labeled before any summary is trusted.

Example · refresh needed
02

Choose the scope

Access to records, wellness signals, research participation, and community features remain separate choices.

No access assumed
03

Keep meaning human

PIXIE displays records and uncertainty. It does not infer emotion, capacity, compliance, crisis, or treatment needs.

Observation ≠ diagnosis
04

Share separately

Nothing goes to a clinician, caregiver, researcher, collaborator, community, or platform without a distinct, reviewable action.

Closed by default

Demonstration boundary: this public interface uses synthetic states only. It contains no personal medical record data and performs no clinical interpretation.
