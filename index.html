The PIXIE promise

Support that respects variable capacity.

01

No punitive streaks

Returning matters more than performing perfectly. A pause is information, not failure.

02

No hidden inference

PIXIE uses preferences a person states. It does not diagnose, infer emotion, or monitor secretly.

03

No trapped choices

Explainable suggestions, explicit permission, reversible actions, and deletion stay within reach.

Consent is an interface

Four visible states.
One person in control.

PIXIE carries Made Sick’s safety grammar into device stewardship. Social contact stays closed by default; sharing and participation are separate choices.

  1. 01
    Recognize

    See a cue or pattern without being enrolled.

  2. 02
    Participate

    Choose the scope, duration, and data involved.

  3. 03
    Transmit

    Share only through a separate, explicit action.

  4. 04
    Withdraw

    Pause, export, correct, or delete without penalty.
